Excel Format

3 sheets required: Categories, Subcategories, Products


Categories: name, description, image

Subcategories: name, category

Products: name, subcategory, shortDescription, sizeOptions, minQuantity, featured, images, filters


sizeOptions: comma-separated — 3x3 inch, 4x4 inch

images: comma-separated filenames — block-01.jpg, block-02.jpg

filters: colon pairs — size:4x4,woodType:teak,shape:square
